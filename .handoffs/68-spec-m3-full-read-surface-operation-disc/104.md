## What was built
- `describe_operation metrics_metrics` now puts a static `guidance` field on its `query` parameter, in the structured output and indented under the query line in the text. The guidance covers:
  - the top-level keys with their JSON types, generated from the validator's own `metricsQueryKeys`;
  - which keys Langfuse requires, the views, the metric, dimension, filter and orderBy item shapes, and the aggregations, granularities and `row_limit` range;
  - one worked example: the total cost per day of the observations of traces named checkout.
- A malformed metrics query (unknown key, wrong key type, not an object, trailing data, over 16 KiB, too deep) is refused with `invalid_argument`. The new hint lists the allowed keys and names `describe_operation`, which for `metrics_metrics` "returns the query's shape and a worked example". Neither text ever holds the query.
- `legacy_metricsV1_metrics` gets no guidance. Its refusal hint lists the keys and says `describe_operation` returns "its parameters".

## Decisions & deviations
- The guidance text lives in `internal/catalog` (`Operation.ParamGuidance`, `HasGuidance`, exported `MetricsQueryKeys`, `ErrMetricsQuery`). That keeps it next to the unexported key map it derives from. The server only renders it.
- `rangeError` was renamed to `markedError`, because it now also carries `ErrMetricsQuery`.
- The unknown key is not echoed at all. That is stricter than "redacted and cut", and it is today's behavior.
- The guidance adds more than the keys and the example: shapes, views, aggregations and granularities from the sourced docs. The key list alone did not tell a model the item shapes.
- The example's closing wording was chosen empirically (4 eval runs; see the raw note). The note "(put the time window asked for …)" made qwen3:8b stop, twice. The shipped wording names the example's week.
