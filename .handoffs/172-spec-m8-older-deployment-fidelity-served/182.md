## What was built
The small-model eval (`scripts/small-model-eval.py`) now prints a `said:` line under an intent whose conversation ends on a model turn with no tool call: the text blocks of that turn as one line, control and format (zero-width, bidi) characters stripped, cut to 500 characters (`...` marks the cut), `(no text)` when the turn has none. It prints for passing intents 12 and 13 too, since they end that way.

With it, one `--only 11` run with the full shipped skill was recorded on `qwen3:8b`, then the one allowed wording change to `skills/langfuse-api-mcp/references/cost-latency.md` was judged on three full runs. Intent 11 failed in all three, so the wording was reverted (the skill is unchanged against the base) and intent 11 failing is recorded as the accepted baseline (ROADMAP M6 and M8 rows, `docs/development/small-model-eval.md`, a dated research record with its index row). #149 closes with the pull request.

## Learnings
- Local eval runs: `scripts/small-model-eval-local.sh --system-append <file>` takes about 3 min for `--only 11` including the container start, about 11 min per full run; three runs back to back in a scratchpad script and a bounded `until` loop on the log work better than the foreground timeout.
- At temperature 0 the same eval inputs give byte-identical output, so three full runs of one text are one sample; a wording change is the only way to get a different one.
- A full `go test -race ./...` can hit the 10 min package timeout when the host load is 40+ (WSL); rerun when the load drops.
